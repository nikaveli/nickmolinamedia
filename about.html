About

I'm Nick.I show up.

I've been a photographer since 2003. Five years ago a friend asked me to shoot the remodels he was building at Summit Custom Builders, and I've been on his job sites ever since. Somewhere in those years it stopped being a favor and became the work I wanted to do.

I added video because photos couldn't carry what it feels like to walk through a finished home. Now I film every project myself, from the first walkthrough to the final edit.

The craftsmanship is already there when I arrive. My job is to make sure the next homeowner sees it.

Based in Denver. Working the Front Range only, so I can always be there in person.
